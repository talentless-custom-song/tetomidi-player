Play MIDI files as key presses in your game

TetoMidi Player reads a MIDI file and presses the piano, drum or guitar keys for you. It has a falling-note view to follow along, hotkeys that work while your game is focused, and a small mini player to keep next to your screen.

Default hotkeys

They work while your game window is focused. You can change them in Settings.

  • Play F2
  • Pause F3
  • Stop F4
  • Speed up F5
  • Slow down F6

Get started

No installer needed. Three steps.

  1. Download the .zipChoose the Windows or Linux version above.
  2. Extract itUnzip into any folder.
  3. Open tetoMIDIClick Open, pick a .mid file, then press Play or F2. On Linux, hotkeys need an X11 session.
